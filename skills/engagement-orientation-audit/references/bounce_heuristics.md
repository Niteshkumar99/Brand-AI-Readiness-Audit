# Post-Click AI-Referral Retention Heuristics

## The AI-Referred Visitor Mindset
A visitor who clicks a citation link inside an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Perplexity) differs fundamentally from a casual browser:
1. **Specific Intent**: They asked the AI assistant a specific question (e.g., *"How much does Acme cost per user?"* or *"Does Acme integrate with Slack?"*).
2. **Immediate Verification Expectation**: They expect to verify the AI assistant's claim within 3-5 seconds.
3. **Near-Zero Friction Tolerance**: If greeted by an intrusive email capture modal, a cookie wall that dims the screen, or a generic marketing slogan with no product details, they click "Back" immediately.

## Key Design Principles to Prevent Bounce
1. **Instant Context Alignment**: The headline and hero paragraph must immediately reinforce the domain's core function.
2. **Clear Information Scent**: Provide unambiguous buttons for "Documentation", "Interactive Demo", "Pricing Calculator", and "Product Specs".
3. **No Unsolicited Interstitials**: Never trigger full-screen modal overlays within the first 10 seconds of user arrival.
